Our Programs & Field Initiatives

Comprehensive educational, health, and climate adaptation programs designed for high-risk coastal environments.

Child Education

1. Sundarbans Learning Hubs & Scholarships

Establishing community-managed learning centers, providing free textbooks, school uniforms, tuition stipends, and teacher training to ensure children complete primary and secondary schooling.

● 15 Active Centers ● 1,250+ Enrolled
Health & Awareness

2. Clean Water & Hygiene Literacy

Conducting hygiene education, installing rainwater harvesting systems, and providing menstrual hygiene kits in salinity-impacted coastal schools.

● 35 Workshops ● 5,000+ Participants
Coastal Livelihood

3. Coastal Climate Preparedness & Youth Action

Training local youth in emergency cyclone preparedness, mangrove reforestation, and community safety protocols during severe weather events.

● 250 Youth Volunteers ● 12 Villages

DINGHY FOUNDATION

Dinghy Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to education, climate awareness, and community empowerment in Satkhira, coastal Bangladesh.
