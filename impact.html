FIELD STORIES

Real Lives Transformed in Satkhira

Case Study: Rahima's Journey to School

Daughter of a coastal fisherman in Kaliganj, 10-year-old Rahima faced dropping out due to cyclone damage. Through Dinghy Foundation's scholarship and learning hub, she is now top of her 4th-grade class.

Case Study: Clean Water in Krishnonagar

Over 500 school children now have daily access to safe drinking water, reducing waterborne illness absences by 62% in 2025.
